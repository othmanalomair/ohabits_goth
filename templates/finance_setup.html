Financial Recommendations

Emergency Buffer

Financial experts recommend keeping 3-6 months of expenses as an emergency buffer. For most people, this is 10-20% of monthly income.

Savings Goals

A good rule of thumb is to save 20% of your income. You can split this between emergency fund, retirement, and personal goals.

Fixed Expenses

Try to keep fixed expenses (rent, loans, subscriptions) under 50% of your income to maintain financial flexibility.
